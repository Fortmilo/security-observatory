Sites & External AccessBaseline Salesforce Site, Experience Cloud and external exposure evidence without claiming deep guest-user coverage.
Entitlements & AssetsPackage Licence Capacity, Permission Set Licence and Salesforce User Licence evidence, plus asset context where approved.

Evidence boundary

Only proven evidence becomes a finding

A missing source, blocked query or unretained detail does not become a zero-risk result. It becomes a visible not-assessed state with a reason and safe next action.

The app never verifies tokens by replaying them and does not describe an OAuth authorisation record as a live or usable token.
