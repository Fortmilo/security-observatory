Coverage state

Two distinct axes

Scanner health is not benchmark outcome

The app separates whether evidence was returned from what the evidence means against SBS. This prevents blocked or missing evidence from being interpreted as a pass.

Evidence returned

The scanner family returned usable evidence at the configured level.

Zero observed

The scanner ran successfully and explicitly observed zero matching records.

Scanner issue

The source was unavailable, blocked or failed and requires verification.

SBS coverage states

Coverage without a compliance claim

  • AutomatedThe Observatory can collect the approved evidence directly.
  • Partial EvidenceUseful evidence exists but does not fully verify the control.
  • Manual RequiredThe control requires human verification.
  • Not CoveredThe current implementation does not assess the control.
  • Extended CheckUseful Observatory evidence exists outside direct SBS control coverage.

SBS alignment supports evidence review. It is not certification, formal compliance scoring or a pass/fail audit.

Supporting tools

Review, compare and export safely

Level-aware comparison

Completed scans can be compared only when both retained the same effective evidence level. Mismatched levels are unavailable, never interpreted as resolved or improved.

Sanitised CSV

Retained evidence and SBS traceability can be exported using shared formula-injection-safe sanitisation.
